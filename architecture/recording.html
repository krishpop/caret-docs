Recording and memory ​

Caret keeps operational state, trajectory evidence and supervisor memory for different purposes. Similar names do not mean interchangeable formats.

From action to record ​

The first v2 act allocates episode and recording-task IDs. The recorder snapshots /ax/state, resolves semantic target descriptions, lifts each executed action into schema-lite vocabulary, and records the post-action state and ranked delta. Intermediate actions use a settle delay (150 ms by default); the last action's post-state follows expectation settling.

menu, focus_window, pressinvokeMenu invocation, raise or accessibility activation

set_value, typeinsert_textReplace or type text; execution tier differs

action.source_action retains the original Pi action. Step outcomes are ok, no_change, or error; these differ from task statuses. Delta entries are capped at 50 with delta_truncated recording the dropped count. Snapshots are content-addressed, so repeated content can share blobs.

A schema is not a populated learning pipeline ​

The task record writer currently emits empty predicate and correction arrays, null intent labels, null success expression and progress, and include_in_training: false. It retains Caret's status in an extension field and maps statuses to the smaller record outcome vocabulary:

Caret statusRecord outcome
successsuccess
partial, needs_userpartial
interruptedabandoned
Other terminal statusesfailed

The correction schema can represent rejected/chosen actions and segments, but this baseline has no automatic live correction writer. Likewise source: shadow and source: teach are schema values, not implemented continuous human-capture modes.

TaskRunner.wait writes task records for eligible tasks with recording state. A resumed task may append another task summary; readers take the final line for its recording task ID. Observation-only tasks do not allocate recording state, and interruption paths that never reach the writer can leave step evidence without a final task record.

Privacy and retention boundaries ​

Secret tasks skip trajectory recording and screenshots. Recording also has a denylist and secure-field handling, but ordinary typed content and original action payloads can be sensitive: do not assume arbitrary values are redacted or encrypted. Export, remote processing and training need their own reviewed data policy.

Successful tasks delete screenshot artifacts from vault/artifacts/<trace>. Content-addressed screenshot blobs referenced by records have a separate lifetime; successful artifact cleanup is not blob garbage collection.

Optional CARET_CU_RECALL=1 uses local finished-task recall to enrich new task cards. This is a separate retrieval feature, not automatic weight training. Full shadow/teach capture, the schema's predicate DAG pipeline and general encrypted sensitive-form handling remain proposals or unimplemented areas in this baseline.
